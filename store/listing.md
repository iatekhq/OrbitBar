## Short title

## Short description
A modern, customizable taskbar for Windows 11. OrbitBar replaces the Windows taskbar with a floating dock: your apps, music with full controls, live system stats, a security shield, camera/mic/location alerts, weather, and study tools that pull deadlines straight from Moodle, Brightspace and Canvas. Free, no ads, no tracking.

## Description
OrbitBar is the taskbar Windows 11 should have shipped with.

It replaces the Windows taskbar with a sleek floating dock (or a full-width bar, top or bottom), and everything on it is live and customizable with a right-click. No registry hacks, no patching Explorer: start OrbitBar and your taskbar is swapped; quit it and the normal one comes back.

YOUR APPS, YOUR WAY
• Pinned apps and open windows, grouped by app, with live window previews on hover
• Drag to reorder, middle-click to open a new window, apps asking for attention pulse amber
• Virtual desktops as pills: click to jump straight to one, middle-click to close it

MUSIC AND MEDIA
• Shows what's actually playing: YouTube, Spotify, browsers and media apps
• Big media card with cover art, live progress, seeking, ±10 s and "play from the start"

SYSTEM AT A GLANCE
• CPU sparkline, memory, GPU (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel), network speed and battery
• Volume on the bar: scroll to change, click for a slider

SECURITY AND PRIVACY, BUILT IN
• Security shield: is Windows Security or your antivirus on, is the firewall on, are definitions up to date
• Network safety chip: VPN on/off, warnings on open or WEP Wi-Fi
• Camera, microphone and location badges show which app is using them, right now
• App permissions: see which apps you allowed or blocked for camera, mic and location

MADE FOR STUDENTS
• Paste your Moodle, Brightspace/D2L, Canvas, Blackboard or Google Classroom calendar link: next class, deadlines and exam countdowns appear on the bar
• Focus mode with Pomodoro timer, gentle nudges away from distractions, and study stats that stay on your PC

AND MORE
• Weather with hourly and daily forecast, calendar and world clocks, notifications, Recycle Bin, quick launcher
• Glass, Midnight, Black and White styles, accent colours, sizes from compact to huge
• Widget gallery: add, remove and rearrange widgets by dragging, changes apply instantly
• Stays put through Win+D, hides for fullscreen games and videos, and brings the Windows taskbar back if anything goes wrong

PRIVACY
OrbitBar has no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything it shows is read on your PC and stays there. It only goes online for weather, update checks and the calendar links you add.

OPEN CODE
OrbitBar is free, and its source code is public, made by IATEK, a cybersecurity bootcamp and consultancy in Toronto. It started as a teaching project: the whole app is one readable Python file. See the code at github.com/iatekhq/OrbitBar.
